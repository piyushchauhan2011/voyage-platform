Spring AI lab

Chat, embeddings, vector search, RAG, tools, agent

Build up a hotel assistant one concept at a time, using Gemini through Spring AI and pgvector for storage. The target question is "Find hotels near beach under $100" — half of it is fuzzy, half of it is arithmetic, and each half needs a different mechanism. Compare with /ui/rabbitmq.

Suggested path

  • Login as ADMIN, then check Status for your Gemini key.
  • Seed catalog to create hotels with descriptions.
  • Try Chat — note it invents hotels.
  • Embed two phrases and compare the scores.
  • Ingest, then Search for "near the sand".
  • RAG, then finally Ask assistant.
